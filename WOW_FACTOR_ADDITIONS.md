## 🎨 Visual Psychology Enhancements

### Attention-Grabbing Animations

1. **Glowing emphasis on hero headline**
   - Pulsing glow on "working product"
   - Eye naturally drawn to movement

2. **Animated trust indicators**
   - Pulsing green dot for availability
   - Creates sense of "live" presence

3. **Interactive code showcase**
   - Tabs that respond to clicks
   - Makes portfolio interactive, not passive

4. **Magnetic card effects**
   - Cards respond to hover
   - Creates sense of quality and attention to detail

5. **Floating gradient orbs**
   - Background movement without distraction
   - Adds depth and premium feel

### Color Psychology Used

- **Red (Accent):** Action, urgency, passion
- **Green:** Trust, success, "go ahead"
- **Dark sections:** Creates contrast, makes other sections pop
- **White space:** Breathing room, clarity, premium feel
